Catchphrase audition — 1,848 clips from the Wii disc

Each clip was transcribed by offline speech recognition and scored against the known catchphrase, best first — so you can usually read the right one rather than hunt for it. Transcripts are rough (“Fear the Fish” comes out as “fair enough fish”), and the European disc mixes six languages in, which show up as nonsense text. Click a row to play, pick to choose.
