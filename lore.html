Skylanders Universe Lore Guide

An official chronicle of Skylands history, elemental cosmology, game eras, and prominent factions.

The Core of Light & Portal Masters

Skylands is a universe composed of vast floating islands suspended in a magical sky. Harmony was preserved for millennia by the Core of Light, an ancient monument powered by the ten fundamental elements. Human Portal Masters from Earth channel elemental energy to bridge realms, summoning Skylanders figures to defend the world against dark forces.

📜 Chronological Eras (2011 – 2016)

1. Spyro's Adventure — The Shattered Core 2011

The dark Portal Master Kaos destroys the Core of Light using his magical hydra, banishing the Skylanders to Earth in a frozen, miniature state. Guided by Master Eon, Earth's Portal Master recovers the elemental source crystals to restore the Core and defeat Kaos.

2. Giants — The Arkeyan Awakening 2012

Kaos unearths the ancient Fist of Arkus on Earth to command the legendary mechanical Arkeyan Empire. The Skylanders Giants—ancient heroes banished ten thousand years prior—are recalled to crush the mechanical armada.

3. Swap Force — Mount Cloudbreak 2013

During Mount Cloudbreak's centennial magical eruption, Kaos attempts to corrupt Skylands with Evilized magic. Caught in a magical explosion, an elite team gains the ability to swap top and bottom halves, creating the SWAP Force.

4. Trap Team — Cloudcracker Prison Break 2014

Kaos detonates Cloudcracker Prison, composed entirely of Traptanium, releasing the notorious Doom Raiders. Skylanders armed with Traptanium weapons hunt down escaped villains and capture them using elemental Traptanium traps.

5. SuperChargers — The Sky-Eater 2015

Kaos constructs the massive Doomstation of Ultimate Doomstruction (the Sky-Eater). Master Eon calls upon the SuperChargers to pilot land, sea, and sky vehicles equipped with rift engines to destroy the station.

6. Imaginators — Mind Magic & Creation 2016

Kaos discovers Mind Magic—the ancient power used by the Ancients to create Skylands—to spawn Doomlanders. Master Senseis train a new generation of custom Imaginator heroes to safeguard creation magic.

🏰 Key Factions & Figures

Master Eon

The last Portal Master of Skylands. Transformed into a spirit when the Core of Light was destroyed, Eon guides real-world Portal Masters to command Skylanders.

Kaos & Glumshanks

An arrogant dark Portal Master seeking supreme dominion over Skylands. His relentless ambition and dark magic make him Skylands' primary recurring adversary.

The Doom Raiders

A villainous alliance led by the Golden Queen, including Wolfgang, Chompy Mage, Chef Pepper Jack, Dr. Krankcase, and Dreamcatcher.

The Arkeyan Empire

An ancient, highly advanced race of giant mechanical beings who ruled Skylands thousands of years ago before being overthrown by the original Giants.
