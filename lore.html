Skylanders Universe Lore Guide

An official chronicle of Skylands history, elemental cosmology, game eras, and prominent factions.

The Core of Light & Portal Masters

Skylands is a universe composed of vast floating islands suspended in a magical sky. Harmony was preserved for millennia by the Core of Light, an ancient monument powered by the ten fundamental elements. Human Portal Masters from Earth channel elemental energy to bridge realms, summoning Skylanders figures to defend the world against dark forces.

Chronological Eras

The history of Skylands spans epochs of peace, conflict, and cosmic upheaval.

The Ancients' Epoch

Pre-History

The Ancients — a highly advanced, enigmatic race — constructed the Core of Light and established the elemental balance. They created the Elemental Gates, the Mask of Power artifacts, and the very fabric of Skylands' magic before vanishing, leaving behind ruins and autonomous constructs like the Arkeyan robots.

The Arkeyan Age

~10,000 Years Ago

The Arkeyan King Jolly and his Iron Fist of Arkus ruled through technological tyranny. Their machine empire enslaved the populace until the Original Skylanders — the Giants — rose up, shattering the Iron Fist and toppling the Arkeyan dynasty. The Giants then vanished, entering a long slumber.

The Dark Ages

~100 Years Ago

With the Giants gone, Kaos rose to power. The Darkness entity corrupted the land, and the Core of Light was destroyed by Kaos and the Hydra. Master Eon called upon a new Portal Master from Earth, beginning the modern era of Skylanders: Spyro's Adventure.

The Modern Era

Present Day

Through Spyro's Adventure, Giants, SWAP Force, Trap Team, SuperChargers, and Imaginators, Skylands has faced Kaos, The Darkness, Malefor, and the Doom Raiders. The Core of Light was restored, the Giants awakened, Traptanium harnessed, vehicles forged, and Imaginite crystals unlocked — each era expanding the roster of Skylanders and the lore of the elements.

The Ten Elements

Fundamental forces that compose all magic in Skylands. Every Skylander aligns with one element, determining strengths, weaknesses, and elemental zones.

Fire

Aggressive, destructive force. Skylanders: Ignitor, Eruptor, Sunburn, Smolderdash, Torch, Wildfire, Barbella.

Water

Fluid, adaptive magic. Skylanders: Gill Grunt, Zap, Wham-Shell, Thumpback, Tidal Wave, Wash Buckler, Bubble Bath.

Earth

Solid, enduring power. Skylanders: Crusher, Bash, Flashwing, Hot Head, Wallop, Rock Jaw, Tri-Tip.

Air

Swift, evasive currents. Skylanders: Sonic Boom, Warnado, Lightning Rod, High Five, Freeze Blade, Scratch, Gusto.

Life

Growth, healing, nature. Skylanders: Stealth Elf, Stump Smash, Shroomboom, Bushwack, Tuff Luck, Brawl & Chain, Aurora.

Undead

Death, shadows, necromancy. Skylanders: Hex, Chop Chop, Cynder, Fright Rider, Night Shift, Funny Bone, Hood Sickle.

Tech

Machinery, innovation, gadgets. Skylanders: Drill Sergeant, Drobot, Boom Jet, Jawbreaker, Fryno, Sprocket, Gearshift.

Magic

Arcane, reality-bending. Skylanders: Spyro, Double Trouble, Voodood, Pop Fizz, Enigma, Dreamcatcher, Mysticat.

Light

Radiance, purity, revelation. Skylanders: Spotlight, Knight Light, Sirious, Amperage, Splatter Splasher.

Dark

Void, stealth, corruption. Skylanders: Blackout, Bat Spin, Pain-Yatta, Star Strike, Deja Vu, Chill Bill, Mesmeralda.

Key Factions

Major organizations and groups that shape the politics and conflicts of Skylands.

Skylanders

The heroic protectors of Skylands, summoned by Portal Masters from Earth. Each Skylander wields one of the ten elements and possesses unique abilities. They are organized by game debut: Core, Giants, SWAP Force, Trap Masters, SuperChargers, Senseis, and Imaginators.

The Doom Raiders

Kaos's elite villain team from Trap Team, each representing an element twisted to darkness: Wolfgang (Air), Dreamcatcher (Magic), Chef Pepper Jack (Fire), Luminous (Light), Shield Shredder (Tech), Golden Queen (Earth), Chompies (Life), Tussle Sprout (Water), Eye-Small (Undead), and Pain-Yatta (Dark).

Arkeyans

Ancient robotic civilization that once ruled Skylands through the Iron Fist of Arkus. Their ruins dot the landscape, and their automatons still guard forgotten vaults. The Giants shattered their empire, but Arkeyan tech persists in traps, vehicles, and Imaginators.

The Darkness

The primordial entity of entropy and void, sealed away by the Ancients. It corrupts all it touches — Kaos, Malefor, the Hydra. Its goal: unmake the Core of Light and return Skylands to nothingness. Only the combined elemental power of Skylanders can repel it.

Mabu & Villagers

The peaceful inhabitants of Skylands — Mabu, Drow, Elves, Cyclopses, and more. They build towns, tend farms, and support Skylanders with shops, upgrades, and quests. Notable settlements: Woodburrow, Skylanders Academy, Ruins of the Ancients, and the various elemental realms.

Portal Masters

Humans from Earth who wield the Portal of Power to summon Skylanders. The most famous is the player — the "Portal Master" — guided by Master Eon. Other Portal Masters include Flynn, Cali, Hugo, Persephone, and Tessa, each with their own connection to the Portal network.
