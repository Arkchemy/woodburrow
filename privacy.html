Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Zero Local Telemetry: Offline modes (Traditional Mode and Hybrid Mode) collect zero telemetry or user data. This policy strictly governs opt-in network services for Skylanders Online.

1. Data Collection Scope

When participating in Skylanders Online, only the minimal technical data required for networking is stored:

  • Display Username: Chosen by you and visible in multiplayer lobbies.
  • Email Address: Used strictly for account recovery and essential notifications.
  • Account Secret Keys: Cryptographic tokens stored locally to authenticate account ownership without storing plain text passwords.
  • Gameplay Locker State: Unlocked figure progression, cosmetics, and shared collection statistics.

2. Privacy Guarantees

  • We never sell or monetize your personal data or gameplay telemetry.
  • We do not expose IP addresses or private network identifiers to other players in multiplayer sessions.
  • We never request real-world identification documents or age verification records.

3. Right to Erasure (Account Deletion)

You may request complete account deletion at any time by contacting us on Discord or emailing aaron@aaronworld.uk.
