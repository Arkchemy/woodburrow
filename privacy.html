Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Zero Local Telemetry: Offline modes (Traditional Mode and Hybrid Mode) collect zero telemetry or user data. This policy strictly governs opt-in network services for Skylanders Online.

1. Data Controller

Bramble Project ("Project", "we", "us") is the data controller for any personal data processed through Skylanders Online network services. Contact: aaron@aaronworld.uk

The offline Software (Bramble recompiler, runtime shims, generated binaries) runs entirely on your device and transmits no data to any server.

2. Offline Modes — No Data Collection

When using Traditional Mode or Hybrid Mode:

  • No analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics are collected.
  • No unique identifiers are generated or transmitted.
  • No network connections are initiated by the Software.
  • Figure data read from Portal of Power or local dumps never leaves your device.
  • Save files, screenshots, and configuration remain local.

Your gameplay is completely private. We cannot see what you play, when you play, or which figures you own.

3. Skylanders Online — Opt-In Network Services

Skylanders Online is a planned future service. This section describes intended practices; final details will be published before launch.

Account Registration (Optional): To use multiplayer features, you may create an account. We will collect:

  • Email address (for verification, password recovery, security notices).
  • Chosen display name (publicly visible in lobbies).
  • Hashed password (bcrypt/argon2, never stored in plaintext).
  • Optional: avatar, bio, friend code.

Gameplay Data (During Active Sessions):

  • Transient session state: lobby membership, character selection, position, actions.
  • Match results: win/loss, score, duration (for leaderboards if enabled).
  • Chat messages (text/voice) — processed for delivery only, not stored long-term.

Technical Data:

  • IP address (for connection routing, DDoS mitigation, geo-location for server selection).
  • Client version, platform, connection quality metrics.
  • Error logs (anonymized) for debugging.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

  • Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): Providing multiplayer matchmaking, friend lists, chat.
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): Security monitoring, abuse prevention, service improvement.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Optional profile fields, marketing emails, leaderboard participation.

You may withdraw consent at any time via account settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared only:

  • With infrastructure providers (VPS, CDN, DDoS protection) under Data Processing Agreements.
  • With moderation staff for enforcement of Code of Conduct.
  • If required by law (court order, legal obligation).
  • In aggregated, anonymized form for public statistics (e.g., "500 active players today").

No data is shared with Activision, Microsoft, Nintendo, or any franchise rights holders.

6. Retention

  • Account data: retained while account exists; deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
  • Session gameplay data: discarded within 24 hours of session end.
  • Chat logs: not persisted beyond delivery; moderation flags retained 90 days.
  • IP logs: retained 14 days for security, then anonymized.
  • Error logs: retained 90 days, no personal identifiers.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — delete your account and associated data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — limit processing of your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise these rights, email aaron@aaronworld.uk. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

  • TLS 1.3 for all network traffic.
  • Passwords hashed with argon2id (configurable cost).
  • Session tokens: short-lived, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict.
  • Rate limiting, IP-based abuse detection, automatic ban on credential stuffing.
  • Regular dependency audits; minimal attack surface (no database exposed to internet).

9. Children's Privacy

Skylanders Online is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If a parent discovers a child has created an account, contact us for immediate deletion.

10. International Transfers

Servers are hosted in the UK/EU. If processing occurs outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions. No data is transferred to the US under Privacy Shield (invalidated).

11. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted here with a revised date. Material changes (new data categories, new purposes) will be notified via email and in-client banner 30 days before taking effect. Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance.
