Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-17

This is a first draft, written to capture the project's real, intended data practices clearly — it has not yet had independent legal review, and Skylanders Online (the only part of Bramble that would collect account data) is not a live service yet.

What this covers

Bramble's software itself (Traditional Mode, Hybrid Mode) runs entirely on your own console and doesn't send any data anywhere. This policy covers Skylanders Online specifically — the only part of the project that involves an account or any data being sent to us.

What we collect

  • Username — chosen by you, shown to other Skylanders Online players.
  • Email address — used for account recovery and account-deletion verification (see below). Not shown to other players.
  • Account creation date/time and a private account key — used only to verify you own an account, e.g. for account-deletion requests. Both are hidden by default and only visible to you, in your own account settings, until you choose to reveal them.
  • Gameplay data — your Skylanders collection, unlocks, progress, and similar data needed for Skylanders Online's shared-locker/cross-game features to work.

We never ask for, and never need, your account password for support, verification, or any other purpose. If anyone claiming to represent Bramble asks for your password, it isn't really us — see the Terms of Service for why sharing it is risky beyond just this project.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to third parties.
  • We don't display private information (like IP addresses) in any in-game network status screens.
  • We don't require real-world identity information to create or use an account.

Deleting your account/data

Contact us via the Bramble Discord server or aaron@aaronworld.uk with your Skylanders Online username, and one of the following as proof the account is yours:

  • Your account's private key or its private account-creation date/time (both found in your own account settings — you only need to provide one of the two), or
  • A request sent from the email address registered to the account — we'll send a confirmation code to that address, which you'll need to provide back to us to confirm you have access to it.

We will never ask for your password as part of this process.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change in the future, in step with the project's own Terms of Service. Continuing to use Skylanders Online after a change means you accept the version in effect at the time.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or data requests, can be sent to aaron@aaronworld.uk or the Bramble Discord server — the only official contact points for this project.
