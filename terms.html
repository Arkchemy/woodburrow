Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-08-17

This is a first draft, written to capture the project's real, intended policies clearly — it has not yet had independent legal review, and Bramble/Skylanders Online are not live services yet. Treat this as a strong statement of intent, not a finished legal document, until it says otherwise.

1. Scope

These Terms apply to Bramble's software, to any online mode or service it operates (referred to below as "Skylanders Online" — including its main mode, Sky Stones, and Skylanders Battle Cast, and anything else labeled as part of Skylanders Online in the future), and to the official Bramble Discord server. Joining the Discord server means you agree to these Terms, which are also linked from the server's own rules.

Traditional Mode and Hybrid Mode are fully offline and are not part of "Skylanders Online" for the purposes of these Terms — see Section 5.

2. What Bramble is

Bramble is a static recompilation tool: it transforms your own, legally-obtained copy of a game you already own into a form that runs on different hardware. Bramble does not contain, host, or distribute any copyrighted game assets, code, or content. You are responsible for legally owning and dumping your own copy of any game you use with Bramble, and for separately complying with that game's own publisher's terms (e.g. Activision, for the Skylanders series).

3. Free, non-commercial, no paywalls

Bramble and every mode/service it operates are provided entirely free of charge. No feature is ever placed behind a paywall or made exclusive to paying supporters. Voluntary donations (e.g. a QR code in-app, GitHub Sponsors) support the project only and grant no gameplay advantage. Bramble itself may not be sold, commercialized, or distributed for payment — see the project's own LICENSE for the full terms governing the software itself.

4. Accounts (Skylanders Online)

  • You're responsible for keeping your account secure. Never share your account password with anyone — Bramble staff will never ask for it, and since many people reuse passwords across sites, sharing it can put your other accounts at risk too.
  • Account sharing and account merging are not allowed, except by contacting us directly to arrange a merge.
  • Selling or trading accounts, or any Skylanders Online currency or unlock, for real-world money or any other platform's virtual currency (e.g. V-Bucks, in-game coins from other games), is not allowed.

5. Fair play & moderation

We reserve the right to suspend (including permanently) or otherwise act on Skylanders Online accounts, at our discretion, for: cheating; modifying client files (intentionally or not) to unlock or gain content outside the normal game flow; toxic behavior toward the community; selling/trading accounts or currency as described in Section 4; or other violations of these Terms.

These moderation actions apply only to Skylanders Online (as scoped in Section 1). A Skylanders Online suspension does not limit your use of Traditional Mode or Hybrid Mode in any way — those modes are fully offline, and you're free to do things there (like giving your own Skylanders extra levels, or using whatever nickname you like) that wouldn't be allowed in Skylanders Online, since they never touch its shared ecosystem.
