Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Zero Local Telemetry

The offline Software collects zero telemetry or user data. This policy strictly governs this website itself and the opt-in, not-yet-released "Skylanders Online" network service.

1 Data Controller

The Bramble Project is the data controller for personal data processed through this website and, in future, Skylanders Online network services. Contact: aaron@aaronworld.uk. The offline Software (the recompiler and anything it produces) runs entirely on your own device and transmits no data to any server.

2 The Offline Software — No Data Collection

  • No analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics are collected by the recompiler or anything it produces.
  • No network connections are initiated by the Software itself.
  • Figure data read from a Portal of Power or local dump never leaves your device.
  • Your gameplay is completely private. We cannot see what you play.

3 This Website

This site's Downloads and License pages call the public, unauthenticated GitHub API directly from your browser to display live repository statistics, release listings, and license text — no personal data is sent as part of those requests, and GitHub's own privacy policy governs what GitHub itself logs about that request. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts of its own.

4 Skylanders Online (Future, Opt-In)

Skylanders Online is a planned, not-yet-released, opt-in multiplayer layer. When it launches, this policy will be updated with the final details, but the intended scope is:

  • Account basics: email address, chosen display name, hashed password.
  • Transient session state: lobby membership, character selection, in-match positions.
  • IP addresses, kept only as needed for connection routing and abuse/DDoS mitigation.

No data is ever shared with Activision, Microsoft, Nintendo, or any other franchise rights holder, and nothing is sold. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any account data by emailing the contact above.
