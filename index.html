Native Skylanders Recompilation for ARM64

Bramble converts original Wii U binary executables directly into native ARM64 instructions for handheld platforms — delivering high performance without runtime CPU emulation.

Architectural Play Modes

Designed to support both original physical hardware collections and modern portable gameplay.

Traditional Mode

A 100% faithful port of the original Wii U releases. Connects directly to real USB Portals of Power and physical NFC figures exactly as released.

Physical Toy Support 100% Faithful

Hybrid Mode

Enhances offline play with modern balance refinements, visual options, and an integrated virtual figure locker for portal-free gaming on the go.

Virtual Figure Locker Portable Play

Skylanders Online

An opt-in shared-world network layer. Connect with friends, explore levels together, trade unlocked variants, and compete without paywalls.
