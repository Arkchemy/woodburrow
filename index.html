Bramble is a static recompilation project bringing Skylanders games to native ARM64 Nintendo Switch homebrew — no emulation at runtime. It transforms your own, legally-owned copy of a game into a form that runs on different hardware, without containing or distributing any of that game's own copyrighted content.

This project is currently in active, early development. Nothing described below (including "Skylanders Online") is available yet — this site exists to document the project's goals, terms, and policies as they're decided, ahead of anything actually shipping.

What Bramble is

  • Traditional Mode — a native, faithful port of the original game, played through your own real (or virtual) Portal of Power and figures, exactly as it originally shipped.
  • Hybrid Mode — Traditional Mode plus quality-of-life/balance improvements and a portal-free unlock system, without any online/social features.
  • Skylanders Online — an opt-in, fully free, accessible shared-presence layer: see other real players in your level, party up with friends, unlock Skylanders without needing physical figures, and more — built without needing to preserve the original toy-sales incentives of the real games.

Free, always

Bramble and every mode/service it operates (including Skylanders Online) are 100% free to use. No feature is ever paywalled or made exclusive to paying supporters — voluntary donations support the project only and confer no in-game advantage.

Legal

Bramble contains no copyrighted game assets or code, and distributes none — it only transforms a copy of a game you already legally own. Use of Bramble is additionally subject to the original game publisher's (e.g. Activision, for the Skylanders series) own terms. See the project's LICENSE for full source-usage terms.
